Themes

Life-cycle assessment and optimisation

Most environmental accounting for pavements stops at construction, where the material tonnage is easiest to count. The use stage and the end-of-life stage are where the larger share of impact often sits, so my work carries the assessment across the full life cycle and treats structural and functional performance as inputs to the environmental result rather than separate questions.

Pervious concrete and stormwater

Permeable pavements trade some structural capacity for drainage, which makes them a stormwater management tool as much as a surfacing choice. Alongside comparative life-cycle assessment of pervious systems, I have worked on the construction detail — including a framework for setting riser strip height rationally rather than by rule of thumb.

Alternative cementitious materials

Cement is the single largest carbon term in most concrete pavements. Geopolymers and other alternative binders offer a route to lowering that footprint, provided the performance case is made on the same evidence as conventional mixes rather than on the strength of the sustainability claim alone.
