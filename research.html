Themes

Circularity in tyre waste

India discards hundreds of millions of tyres a year, and where they end up decides whether that carbon is recovered or released. My work models the recycling chain itself as the variable — what changes when the plants doing the shredding and pyrolysis run on renewable power instead of grid coal.

Pervious pavement systems

Permeable pavements trade structural capacity for drainage. I compare conventional pervious concrete against multilayered all-weather systems on lifecycle terms, and have worked on the construction detail — including a framework for setting riser strip height rationally rather than by rule of thumb.

Appraisal parameters

Lifecycle cost analysis is only as honest as the discount rate behind it. A road built now is paid for by people who cannot yet vote on it, which makes the social discount rate an ethical parameter as much as a financial one.
